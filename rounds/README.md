# Rounds

This folder is about the **format** of a round, not the subject matter. The
technical content lives in [ai/](../ai/), [dsa/](../dsa/) and
[system-design/](../system-design/). What is here is how the hour is run, what
the grader is filling in, and how to handle the shape of it.

## Before you lean on any of this

These are **reported** formats, pieced together from what candidates have
described. Companies change their loops, run pilots, and vary them by team,
level and location. Anything written down about a loop starts going stale the
day it is written.

So ask your recruiter what your loop actually contains. They will usually tell
you, in detail, and it is a normal question. Ask how many rounds, what each one
is, whether AI tools are allowed in the coding round, and whether there is a
take-home. An hour of prep aimed at the wrong format is worse than no prep.
